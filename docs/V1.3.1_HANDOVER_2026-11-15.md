## Corrected defects

### Pico processing

Every Sample previously removed exactly one queued packet per acquisition tick, so a 500 Hz producer with a slower consumer could remain permanently behind and eventually present samples older than the 350 ms tracking threshold. Consumption remains chronological while capacity permits, but queued processing snapshots older than 100 ms are now discarded before the next cumulative position is consumed. UART gaps, queue overflows, all processing discards and age-policy rejections are separate counters. Queue oldest age and lifecycle resets are visible in Engineering.

The queue is cleared on disconnect/close, stop, stale-link invalidation, Pico boot-session change, Pico-time rollback and processing-mode change. Those transitions preserve the existing reference-invalidating safety policy and prevent an old-session packet entering new tracking history. Latest Snapshot remains the stable 200 Hz default.

### Graphics diagnostics

GFX, Preview and Control incorrectly used the absent `render_camera.updated_mono`. They now derive pose identity from documented `render_timestamp`, interpolation status and validity. New messages, animation frames, held snapshots, missing identity, transport staleness, invalid tracking and duplicate messages are distinct; unchanged physical coordinates are not treated as missing tracking.

Summaries are posted at most once per five seconds to an in-memory registry limited to 32 page/client pairs with a 30-second TTL. Engineering polls that registry, so a separately identified OBS/laptop browser is visible; `?metrics_name=OBS` supplies a human label. No per-frame telemetry, persistence or on-air overlay was added, and browser/Pi clocks are never subtracted to claim end-to-end latency.

### Power administration

The root-owned mode-0600 Argon2id credential remains protected. Optional systemd `LoadCredential=` supplies the unprivileged service a runtime copy; direct paths remain supported for tests/non-systemd environments. Status distinguishes missing, unreadable, malformed and usable credentials plus helper availability. A normal install without power administration starts with controls disabled.

The root helper reads a root-controlled service-name file and accepts only `kairix-reallines-dev.service` or `kairix-reallines.service`; HTTP cannot choose a unit. Development and production Uvicorn definitions include `--no-proxy-headers`, and power authorization rejects forwarded transport headers plus Host/Origin mismatch. Both service users have narrowly enumerated sudoers entries.

Once a valid action is accepted, a mutation gate stops new writes and drains admitted operations. A helper error clears pending state, reopens mutations and exposes a sanitized failure. Successful helper return is reported as dispatched, not completed. Automated tests use fake adapters only; no real power action was executed.
