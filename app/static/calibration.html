1. Calibrate camera movement

Verify live counts, calibrate gear ratio and direction, then set a fresh camera position in Setup & System. This does not require venue calibration.

Live tracking axes

Calibrate gear ratio and direction

  1. Put the camera at a known starting position.
  2. Click “Mark start”.
  3. Rotate the camera by a known amount, for example 90°.
  4. Enter the signed movement.
  5. Click “Calculate gear ratio”.
  6. Review and apply the result.
  7. Set the camera position again after applying.

Applying this changes the encoder mapping, requires a new encoder position reference, and invalidates incompatible venue calibration.

Advanced / Legacy direction-only calibration

Normally you do not need this. The movement calibration above can determine direction and gear ratio together.

2. Measure the camera on the tripod

Camera position on tripod: measure approximately from the centre of the tripod's Pan/Tilt rotation to the camera's optical centre (roughly the lens position used for tracking).

Lens position relative to tripod pivot

Forward offset cannot be independently recovered from crosshair marks, so its physical measurement remains important.

Calculated camera position

Applying changes the reusable setup profile and requires venue calibration to be calculated again. Position references are preserved.
