Refinement

Saved gold observations are measurements and never move when a refinement is applied. When the camera is still at a saved pose, the blue prediction is drawn against those fixed gold points so any residual error remains visible instead of being hidden. A changed encoder reference invalidates the affected stored observations. The overlay uses the same delayed render_camera as normal graphics. Calibration and ground-grid overlays to GFX are session-only and default OFF after a service restart. A measured rectangle can also define the local venue/world frame: its centre becomes X=0/Y=0, its plane is vertical, and the entered bottom-edge height defines ground Z=0.

Current model limits: RealLines still uses a pinhole lens model with no radial/tangential distortion correction, and the current rig model assumes intersecting Pan/Tilt axes with one optical-centre offset. These are the next precision terms to calibrate for near-pixel-perfect lock.

Advanced: manual Pan/Tilt gearing, direction and IMU calibration

1. Calibrate camera movement

Verify live counts, calibrate gear ratio and direction, then set a fresh camera position in Setup & System. This does not require venue calibration.

Live tracking axes

Calibrate gear ratio and direction

  1. Put the camera at a known starting position.
  2. Click “Mark start”.
  3. Rotate the camera by a known amount, for example 90°.
  4. Enter the signed movement.
  5. Click “Calculate gear ratio”.
  6. Review and apply the result.
  7. Continue testing with the same encoder reference.

Applying mapping changes preserves the live encoder count and position reference, but an existing venue calibration must be verified or recalculated.

Optional: IMU-assisted movement calibration

If an MPU-9250 is enabled, RealLines can measure several short movements. Manual calibration above always remains available. The IMU is used only to prepare a proposal and is not needed during production.

  1. Keep the camera still and measure gyro bias.
  2. Mounting alignment: start Pan, move right, stop; then start Tilt, move up, stop.
  3. For each axis, record at least three short natural movements including a reversal.
  4. Review confidence and explicitly apply the proposal.
