Setup profile

Tracking axes

Pan and Tilt are required for valid tracking. Quadrature references are session-only and must be set after every service restart. PPR means A-channel cycles per encoder revolution: a 600 PPR encoder produces 600/1200/2400 counts per revolution at ×1/×2/×4.

Electrical safety: Raspberry Pi GPIO is 3.3 V only. Do not connect 5–24 V encoder outputs directly. Verify push-pull/open-collector output type and use correct level conversion and pull-ups before connecting hardware. No software debounce is applied by default.
