Setup profile

Pan and Tilt tracking

Choose the tracking source, check that encoder counts move, then set the camera's current position. Position references are session-only and must be set after every service restart.

Electrical safety: Raspberry Pi GPIO is 3.3 V only. Do not connect 5–24 V encoder outputs directly. Verify output type, level conversion and pull-ups before connecting hardware.

Advanced / Development — Simulator inputs

Only axes whose active source is Simulator can be adjusted here. Hardware values are shown in the tracking cards above.

Graphics Output Safety

Testing only. Graphics can be shown before venue calibration, but they may not stay accurately locked to the real world.
