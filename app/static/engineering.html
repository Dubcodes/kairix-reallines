Setup profile

Pico encoder controller

One shared RP2040 PIO decoder for Pan, Tilt and Zoom. Internal and external 4.7 kΩ pull-ups may coexist; encoder A/B pull-ups must terminate at 3.3 V, never 5 V. The UART receiver waits for valid KRP1 packets; no firmware updates are performed automatically.

Pi UART: GPIO15 physical pin 10 receives Pico GP8; Pi GPIO14 physical pin 8 sends to Pico GP9 (reserved); grounds common. USB powers/programs Pico. Use the matched firmware included in this patch. After power-up, reference each axis in Engineering.

Pan, Tilt and Zoom tracking

Choose the tracking source and verify encoder counts. Pan/Tilt references describe camera angles; optional physical Zoom reports relative lens counts and drives live FOV once Wide/Tight endpoints are captured. Position references are session-only and must be set after every service restart.

Electrical safety: Raspberry Pi GPIO is 3.3 V only. Do not connect 5–24 V encoder outputs directly. Verify output type, level conversion and pull-ups before connecting hardware.

Ground Plane Adjustment

Fine correction for ground-attached graphics after proper camera and venue calibration. Original object heights and surveyed venue geometry are not changed.
